# Art Direction — Asterwild

## Style

- Stylized 2D, atmospheric, with depth.
- Parallax layers.
- Dynamic lighting where relevant.
- Fog, particles, weather effects.
- Visually distinct biomes.
- Recognizable creatures, expressive characters.
- Modern coherent UI.

## Constraints

- Not Starbound pixel art.
- Original assets only; never reuse protected Starbound sprites/textures/sounds/data.
- Avoid art requirements needing unrealistic manual production.
- Prefer reusable systems that avoid repetition feel.
- Document asset needs and production conventions.
