# Vision — Asterwild

## Identity

Asterwild is an independent 2D space game of exploration, adventure, construction, survival and discovery.
Spiritual successor to Starbound, but with its own visual identity, mechanics, universe, resources and code.

## Core promise

A vast, living, explorable space universe where every planet has identity, history potential, unique
characteristics and specific reasons to be explored. Not a mere color variation of another planet.

## Player fantasy

- Explore many planets and radically different environments.
- Discover caves, ruins, abandoned structures.
- Gather resources, break and place blocks.
- Build shelters, bases, installations.
- Craft items, equipment, machines.
- Develop the character.
- Fight creatures and adversaries.
- Discover new technologies.
- Build and upgrade a spaceship.
- Travel between star systems.
- Meet civilizations, trade, diplomacy.
- Found colonies, develop infrastructures.
- Discover consequences of decisions and the history of the universe.

## Progression

Non-linear. Exploration, building, trade, diplomacy, survival and combat are all valid play styles.

## Art direction

Stylized 2D, atmospheric, depth, parallax, dynamic lighting where relevant, fog, particles, weather.
Distinct from Starbound pixel art. Original assets only.

## Constraints

- Solo first. Multiplayer possible later, architecture must not forbid it.
- Platforms: Linux and Windows first; macOS not blocked.
- Dev machine: 8 GB RAM. Avoid heavy tools, limit dependencies, keep architecture pragmatic.
- No REA / no reverse engineering of Starbound binary. Study via public docs, wiki, observation.
